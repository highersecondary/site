About the Board

A public examination body for Larkano's affiliated institutions

The Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education, Larkano (BISE Larkano) conducts the Secondary School Certificate (SSC) and Higher Secondary School Certificate (HSSC) examinations for its affiliated schools and colleges. Each syllabus sets out the topics, sub-topics and Student Learning Outcomes a subject covers, and the Scheme of Assessment that governs how it is examined.

Every document on this site follows the same structure — Subject Rationale, Concept Map, Student Learning Outcomes by cognitive level, and a full Scheme of Assessment — so a teacher or student moving between subjects always knows where to look.
